  Session length: ~25 min each.
- **Findings** — 4 concrete rewrite items, 0 blocking bugs:
  1. Onboarding step 3 "Your workspace" tooltip felt redundant → merged into step 2.
  2. "Create your first project" CTA copy read as documentation, not action → changed verb to "Try creating one now."
