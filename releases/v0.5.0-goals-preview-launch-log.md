## What went well

- **T-132/T-133 landing together was the right call.** Trying to ship the loop and then bolt on events would have meant a broken commit in between.
- **The GoalRunner single-flight lock made the 60s cadence safe under slow sinks.** No pileup even in the simulated 5s-per-call PostHog HogQL query worst-case.
- **Sprint 09 carry-overs (T-137, T-138, T-139) took less time than budgeted** because their shape was clear from the retro.

## What went badly

- **The `pin_shown` localStorage keys have no expiry.** A power user with 100+ pin ids over time accumulates 100+ localStorage entries; not a real problem for now (each key is ~40 bytes), but worth flagging for v1.0's GDPR delete API (ADR-0005 already covers namespace-scoped clear).
- **Goal windowMinutes has no per-tour default beyond 60.** For short flows a 60min window over-counts (any project creation in the next hour counts). Sprint 11 could add tour-level guidance in `how-to-use-goals.md` — that doc doesn't exist yet.

## Surprises

- **No support tickets tagged "onboarding stuck" in the launch week.** Goal event fired often enough that PMs stopped assuming the drop-off meant a UX problem. First time in the sprint history that a launch reduced ticket volume in a category adjacent to but not directly targeted by the feature.
- **Adopter Product A's `data-tour` PR merged on Day 54**, ahead of the retro-adjusted estimate. Sprint 11 can start Adopter Product A onboarding immediately instead of sliding to Sprint 12.
